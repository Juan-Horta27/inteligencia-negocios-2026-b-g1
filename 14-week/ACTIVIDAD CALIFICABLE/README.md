## Dashboard & insight

This dashboard analyses the 2025 sales of a coffee roaster and tells its story in three pages: context, finding and recommendation. The first page shows that the company sold about 1.29 billion pesos with a 36.6 % margin and reached 97 % of its budget, with ground coffee as the best-selling category. The second page contains the key insight: capsules account for 15 % of sales but have the lowest margin of the five categories (23.8 % against 36.6 % for the whole company), and they also reached only 86 % of their budget. Two slicers (date range and city), a Category → Product drill-down and a cross-filter from the margin chart to the product chart let the user check that the problem affects the four capsule products, whose margins range from 22.8 % to 25.8 %. The recommendation is that the commercial and purchasing teams renegotiate the capsule cost with the supplier, or raise the list price, to bring the margin up to 30 % within 60 days. With the same sales volume, that change would add roughly 12 million pesos of profit per year, and the dashboard lets the team track the capsule margin every month against that 30 % target.
